FAQs

How does URL shortening work?

URL shortening works by taking a long URL and creating a shorter, condensed version that redirects to the original URL. When a user clicks on the shortened link, they are redirected to the intended destination.

Is it necessary to create an account to use the URL shortening service?

No account is required for basic shortening, but creating one unlocks custom aliases, analytics and link management.

Are the shortened links permanent? Will they expire?

Shortened links remain active as long as the destination URL is valid. Links may be reviewed for abuse or inactivity.

Are there any limitations on the number of URLs I can shorten?

There are no hard limits on the free plan — shorten as many URLs as you need, within fair-use policies.

Can I customize the shortened URLs to reflect my brand or content?

Yes. With custom slugs and branded domains you can make every shortened link reflect your brand.

Can I track the performance of my shortened URLs?

Absolutely. Built-in analytics show clicks, referrers, locations and device breakdowns for every link.

How secure is the URL shortening service? Are the shortened links protected against spam or malicious activity?

All links are screened automatically for spam and malicious content, and suspicious links are disabled promptly.

What is a QR code and what can it do?

A QR code is a scannable barcode that takes people straight to your link — perfect for print, packaging and events.

Is there an API available for integrating the URL shortening service into my own applications or websites?

Yes, a full REST API is available on Professional and Teams plans for integrating shortening into your own apps.
